    The strategy
    
  
    Understanding code happens in four steps. Most people jump straight to the last one and wonder why it hurts.
    
  
      
    
    1. Trace. Read code and guess what it prints before you run it.
      2. 
    
    2. Explain. Say what a method is for in one sentence.
      3. 
    
    3. Write. Blank file, from memory, autocomplete off.
      4. 
    
    4. Build. Put the pieces together into a real app.
      5. 
  
    
  
    Do a bit of each, every day. Read a task, close the page, write it in your IDE. Only open the reference once you have tried, then retype it yourself. Rate honestly, because Shaky comes back on your redo list. Three a day beats twenty on a Sunday.
