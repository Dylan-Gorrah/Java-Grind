    The strategy
    
  
    Understanding code happens in four steps. Most people jump to the last one and wonder why it hurts.
    
  
      
    
    1. TraceRead code and guess what it prints before running it.
      2. 
    
    2. ExplainSay what a method is for in one sentence.
      3. 
    
    3. WriteBlank file, from memory, autocomplete off.
      4. 
    
    4. BuildPut the pieces together into a real app.
      5. 
  
    
  
    A bit of each, most days. Try before you open the reference, then retype it yourself. Rate honestly, because Shaky comes back on your redo list.
